About Me

I am a machine learning researcher with a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the Technion. I previously worked as a Computer Vision and Machine Learning Engineer at Apple, and I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at Columbia University in the Zuckerman Institute.

My work focuses on developing learning-based and statistical methods for complex, noisy, high-dimensional data. Across industry and academic research, I have worked on deep learning, computer vision, generative modeling, multimodal representation learning, probabilistic inference, neural decoding, signal processing, and large-scale data pipelines.

Current research

  • Foundation and multimodal models for large-scale neural recordings
  • Representation learning and diffusion models for denoising and neural signal modeling
  • Statistical inference and decoding from high-dimensional electrophysiology
  • Signal processing, compression, and dimensionality reduction for neural time series

Selected Research Projects

Selected projects spanning large-scale neuroscience, generative modeling, computer vision, and computational imaging.

Brain-Wide Electrophysiology Atlas

A brain-wide reference model of electrophysiological organization built from approximately 1,000 Neuropixels recordings. The project combines electrophysiological, anatomical, molecular, and cellular information to model neural activity across spatial scales and infer recording locations directly from measured activity.

This Microtubule Does Not Exist: Super-Resolution Microscopy Image Generation by a Diffusion Model

A diffusion-based generative model for super-resolution microscopy, with quantitative validation of image realism, diversity, and potential training-data memorization.

Paper

DBlink: Dynamic Localization Microscopy in Super Spatiotemporal Resolution

A deep-learning method for reconstructing dynamic biological processes from single-molecule localization microscopy data with improved spatiotemporal resolution.

Paper Article

One-Click Reconstruction in Single-Molecule Localization Microscopy

Parameter-aware deep learning for robust SMLM reconstruction with reduced manual tuning and improved generalization across imaging conditions.
